Nepal

History

Main article: History of Nepal

Prehistory

Nepal is a Himalayan country in South Asia. Archaeological evidence shows people have lived in the region for tens of thousands of years, and the Kathmandu Valley became a centre of trade and culture between the Indian subcontinent and Tibet.

Over the centuries the valley saw the rise of the Licchavi and Malla kingdoms, followed by the unification of Nepal in the 18th century.

Geography

Nepal lies between China and India and contains eight of the world's ten highest peaks, including Mount Everest.
